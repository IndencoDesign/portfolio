About Indenco

We build things people enjoy using — and that clients are proud to show off.

Indenco Design is built on nearly six years of experience designing and building websites for small businesses, nonprofits, and residential multifamily communities across the country. Every project starts with understanding what you actually need, not what a template already offers.

We care about the details most people skip — typography that breathes, interactions that feel earned, copy that doesn't sound like a robot wrote it. We specialize in code-based development, which delivers faster, more dynamic performance than a generic drag-and-drop layout. Whether you need a fresh brand site, a complete redesign, or a custom e-commerce experience, you'll get the same level of craft.
